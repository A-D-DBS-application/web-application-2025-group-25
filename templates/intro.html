What is Spotable?

Spotable builds AI technology that automatically generates quotes for roofing companies. Our advanced automation system eliminates the manual work involved in creating quotes, helping roofing companies streamline their operations and focus on what matters most—growing their business.

Why Manual Quoting Wastes Resources

Traditional quote creation is a time-consuming process that drains your company's resources. Your team spends countless hours on administrative tasks, traveling to job sites, and measuring roofs manually. These hours add up quickly, reducing your team's capacity to take on more projects and generate additional revenue.

Every hour spent on manual quoting is an hour that could be invested in billable work, customer relationships, or business growth. The hidden costs of manual processes extend beyond just time—they impact your bottom line and limit your scalability.

Administrative Hours

Measurement Hours
